# The Vintage Cube — two-player edition, version 1 (180 cards)

*Designed 2026-10-03 for Justin and a friend: grid or Winston draft, 40-card decks, best of three. Built from the MTGO Vintage Cube (Cube Cobra "mtgovc", version of 2026-08-19, 540 cards), then reshaped for two drafters. Every name verified on Scryfall and checked against Forge 2.0.14's card database. Chosen on gameplay alone; price was not a factor, and anything not owned gets proxied.*

## What it is

The Vintage Cube's power and its iconic cards (Black Lotus, the Moxen, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, Tinker, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell, Reanimate, Jace, Oko), cut down to the 180 cards that make eight decks work. In a 540-card cube each archetype is spread thin and you hope to see its pieces. Here two players see 162 of the 180 cards in a grid draft, so every archetype has to be dense enough that both drafters reliably end up with a real deck, and each degenerate card needs an answer somewhere in the pool.

## Design principles

1. **Eight archetypes, deep enough for two.** Each deck has 15–25 cards that want to be in it and a pair of gold signposts. Most cards sit in two or three themes, so a deck that loses a fight over one card can pivot without losing its earlier picks.
2. **Iconic power, spread out.** Eight of the Power Nine are in, plus Sol Ring. Each Mox is filed under its own colour (as on MTGO), so the five fast-mana artifacts land in five different decks instead of one artifact pile. Timetwister, Mana Crypt, Mana Vault, Mishra's Workshop, Library of Alexandria, Bazaar of Baghdad, Tolarian Academy, Channel, Fastbond and Oath of Druids are out (see "Power choices").
3. **Cheating big things is a deck, not a single card.** Reanimate, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell, Tinker and Natural Order all need two or three cards to work (enabler, target, discard outlet). Each one is matched by cheap interaction from the other colours: Force of Will, Mana Drain, Thoughtseize, Hymn, Swords, Containment Priest, Scavenging Ooze, Endurance, Deathrite Shaman and fast pressure from aggro.
4. **No turn-two infinite combos.** Blightsteel Colossus, Thassa's Oracle, Doomsday, Underworld Breach + Lion's Eye Diamond, Painter's Servant + Grindstone, Dark Depths + Thespian's Stage, Kiki-Jiki, Displacer Kitten, Hullbreacher, Thopter Foundry + Sword of the Meek, Tendrils and Brain Freeze are all left out. The strongest starts left in (Lotus into Sneak Attack, turn-one Entomb + Reanimate) are the iconic Vintage experience, and each one loses to a single counter, discard spell or removal spell.
5. **Every colour can fight.** Each colour has cheap removal or counters: Swords/Solitude/Council's Judgment, Force of Will/Mana Drain/Counterspell, Fatal Push/Bowmasters/Toxic Deluge, Bolt/Fury/Pyrokinesis, Beast Within/Ooze/Endurance. Aggro decks have fair answers to bombs, and control decks can actually control.
6. **Real mana for two-colour plus a splash.** The ten original duals and ten fetchlands: twenty fixing lands, so about ten per drafter. Fetches also feed the land and graveyard themes, and Ancient Tomb speeds up Sneak, Show and Tinker.

## The themes and how they connect

| Code | Theme | Core idea | Connects to |
|---|---|---|---|
| AGG | Aggro | Cheap efficient threats, haste, burn to finish | ART, SPL, SAC |
| SPL | Spells / tempo | Cantrips, prowess, delve, cheap instants that fuel creatures | AGG, GY, CTRL |
| CTRL | Control | Counterspells, sweepers, card advantage, planeswalkers | SPL, ETB, RAMP |
| GY | Graveyard | Discard outlets, self-mill, delve, flashback and recursion | REAN, SPL, SAC, LND |
| REAN | Cheat into play | Reanimate, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell, Tinker, Natural Order, Survival | FAT, GY, RAMP |
| FAT | Big payoffs | The creatures and artifacts worth cheating, which also win when hard-cast late | REAN, ETB, RAMP |
| ART | Artifacts / equipment | Moxen, cheap artifacts, equipment, artifact payoffs, Tinker targets | RAMP, AGG, REAN |
| RAMP | Fast mana | Moxen, rituals, mana creatures, Sol Ring | every big plan |
| LND | Lands | Fetchlands, landfall, land recursion, lands as resources | GY, RAMP, SAC |
| ETB | Enter / leave value | Value creatures, blink, evoke elementals | REAN, CTRL, SAC |
| SAC | Sacrifice / tokens | Token makers, sac outlets, recursive fodder | AGG, GY, ETB |

Lower-case words (removal, counter, discard) mark interaction and aren't themes.

**Hub cards** that sit in three or more themes and let you change lanes: Grief (ETB/REAN/GY), Fury (ETB/REAN/AGG), Archon of Cruelty (FAT/ETB/REAN), Recurring Nightmare (REAN/ETB/SAC), Faithless Looting (GY/REAN/SPL), Frantic Search (GY/RAMP/SPL), Fable of the Mirror-Breaker (ART/SAC/GY), Third Path Iconoclast (SPL/ART/SAC), Uro (LND/GY/RAMP), Seasoned Pyromancer (GY/SAC/SPL), Kolaghan's Command (GY/ART/removal), Ancient Tomb (RAMP for every cheat plan).

How they pair off in a two-player draft: the cheat decks (3, 4, 5, 7) share enablers and targets, so one drafter can stay in "cheat" while switching between Reanimator and Sneak. The fair decks (1, 2, 6, 8) are where the cheap interaction lives, so a fair drafter is naturally the cheat drafter's foil. Gold signposts exist for all ten pairs so off-plan pairs (GW creatures, BG midrange) are real fallbacks rather than trainwrecks.

## Power choices

- **In: Black Lotus, Ancestral Recall, Time Walk, all five Moxen, Sol Ring.** These are the Vintage cube; leaving them out makes it a Legacy cube. With grid drafting both players see them, so taking the Lotus costs the rest of that row. Moxen are filed by colour so they reward commitment rather than all landing in one deck.
- **Out: Timetwister.** It's the weakest of the Nine in a 1v1 40-card game and would make blue three-for-three on Power. It's the first card to add back if you want the full set.
- **Out: Mana Crypt, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Mishra's Workshop.** A second and third colorless Sol Ring go into every deck and widen the turn-one gap past what interaction can bridge. Ancient Tomb stays because it costs life and mostly powers the cheat decks.
- **Out: Library of Alexandria, Bazaar of Baghdad, Tolarian Academy, Gaea's Cradle, Strip Mine, Wasteland.** Library decides 1v1 games alone. Bazaar and Academy only work in decks that aren't in this list. Land destruction is miserable with 20 fixing lands across two players.
- **Out: Channel, Fastbond, Oath of Druids, Doomsday, Flash, Tendrils/Brain Freeze, Yawgmoth's Will, Underworld Breach, Thassa's Oracle, Blightsteel Colossus, Hullbreacher, Displacer Kitten, Nadu.** Each is either a turn-one/two win or a narrow combo that needs a drafted shell this cube doesn't support.
- **In and justified (two-card "I win" starts):**
  - *Sneak Attack or Show and Tell + Emrakul/Griselbrand.* Iconic. Answers before it lands: Force of Will, Mana Drain, Counterspell, Memory Lapse, Thoughtseize, Hymn, Tidehollow Sculler, Containment Priest (exiles any creature that enters without being cast). After it lands: Swords, Infernal Grasp and Fatal Push handle Griselbrand or Archon; Emrakul has protection from coloured spells, so only Solitude's ability, Council's Judgment (doesn't target), Balance, Toxic Deluge and Wrath of God deal with it.
  - *Entomb + Reanimate.* Iconic. Answers: Containment Priest, Scavenging Ooze, Endurance, Deathrite, Swords/Solitude/Jailer, counters, discard.
  - *Tinker + Wurmcoil/Battlesphere/Kaldra.* No Blightsteel, so a turn-one Tinker gives a big threat, not a win. Swords, Council's Judgment, Karn, Oko (elk), Kolaghan's Command and Fiery Confluence answer it.
  - *Natural Order + Craterhoof.* Only wins with a board. Natural Order can't find Emrakul (colorless), so it's a ramp spell for Avenger, Primeval or Titania most games.
  - *Balance.* Brutal with Moxen, but it's symmetrical and asks for a real deck-building decision (stay low on lands and creatures).

## Cards to watch in testing

- **Oko, Thief of Crowns** is the most likely "this pick decided the draft" card. It's in because GU needs a real signpost and grid draft shows it to both players. If it wins every game it's in, swap it for Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student.
- **Containment Priest** exiles any nontoken creature that enters without being cast, so it stops Reanimate, Sneak Attack, Show and Tell, Natural Order and Tinker on Wurmcoil or Battlesphere all at once. That's intended (a 2-mana flash answer to every cheat deck makes those decks play around it), but cut it if the cheat decks stop being playable.
- **Balance** and **Time Walk** are the two cards most likely to feel unfair. Balance with a hand of Moxen is the classic blowout; if it happens often, swap it for Unexpectedly Absent.
- **Skullclamp + Bloodghast / Young Pyromancer** is the strongest fair engine. Intended.
- **Sheoldred, the Apocalypse** is the fair bomb most likely to beat aggro alone. Kept because it's black's answer to the burn decks and a fine reanimation target.
- **Jitte** dominates creature mirrors; if games become Jitte-or-lose, swap for Sword of Fire and Ice.

## How to draft it

Grid draft (18 grids of 9 from a shuffled 162) is recommended: both players see nearly the whole cube, so you can plan around the Power and see which cheat plan the other drafter is building toward. Winston works too and keeps more secrets; with Winston, prioritize the cheat enablers early (they're the scarce half of each combo), since targets come late.

Build 40 cards with 16 lands (15 if you have two or more Moxen/Lotus/Sol Ring), and keep basics on hand. Best of three, sideboarding allowed from your drafted pool: hosers like Containment Priest, Endurance, Scavenging Ooze and Pyrokinesis are meant to come in for games two and three.

## Forge notes

All 180 cards exist in Forge 2.0.14's card database (`res/cardsfolder/cardsfolder.zip`), and all 180 names match Scryfall's canonical names exactly. Double-faced cards are listed by front face, as Forge names them: Delver of Secrets, Jace, Vryn's Prodigy, Brazen Borrower, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Bonecrusher Giant.

Fifteen cards carry an `AI:RemoveDeck` flag, meaning Forge's AI can't play them well. A human can draft and play them normally, and the AI can still cast them in a deck it's handed; it just plays them badly or not at all:

- `AI:RemoveDeck:All` (AI never uses them well): Mystic Confluence, Toxic Deluge, Faithless Looting, Goblin Welder, Goblin Bombardment, Sylvan Library, Beast Within, Mishra's Bauble.
- `AI:RemoveDeck:Random` (excluded from random AI decks): Loran of the Third Path, Frantic Search, Tinker, Demonic Tutor, Sneak Attack, Survival of the Fittest, Ancient Tomb (Goblin Welder has both flags).

When a Forge AI opponent drafts or is handed a deck, give it the fair decks (white aggro, Izzet tempo, sacrifice, ramp). The cheat decks lean on flagged cards (Tinker, Sneak Attack, Survival, Faithless Looting, Goblin Welder) and are meant for human pilots.

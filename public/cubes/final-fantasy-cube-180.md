# The Final Fantasy Cube — version 1 (180 cards, two-player)

*Designed 2026-10-10 for Justin and a friend: Grid draft (or Winston or booster), 40-card decks, best of three. Every card is from the Final Fantasy release (June 2025): the main set, its Commander decks, and the Through the Ages cards that came in its boosters. Every name was checked on Scryfall. Intended as a full-proxy cube; Justin owns about a third of it (Counter Blitz and the two Starter Kits).*

## Source and credit

The picks are Claude's, made for fun, synergy and solid two-player games, and guided by data: every main-set and Through the Ages card was ranked by its games-in-hand win rate in 769,659 Premier Draft games of Final Fantasy on MTG Arena, from 17Lands' public game data (`game_data_public.FIN.PremierDraft`, last updated 2025-07-19; 17Lands, CC BY 4.0, credited in NOTICE). That ranking was the starting point, not the rule: a few strong cards were left out for being dull or warping (Atraxa, Ragavan, Yawgmoth), and a few lower-ranked ones are in because an archetype needs them (Tidus, Garnet). Three cards come from the Commander decks, which have no draft data, and were chosen by reading them.

Through the Ages cards in the cube (10): Adeline, Resplendent Cathar, Captain Lannery Storm, Counterspell, Cryptic Command, Deadly Dispute, Fatal Push, Fynn, the Fangbearer, Lightning Bolt, Smuggler's Copter, Venser, Shaper Savant. From the Commander decks (3): Auron, Venerated Guardian, Summon: Ixion, Summon: Valefor.

## Design principles

1. **The set's own ten archetypes.** Final Fantasy was designed for draft: each colour pair has a plan and gold signposts (two or three each here), so a drafted deck has a direction from the first grid.
2. **Its mechanics, at their best.** Job select Equipment (each one brings a 1/1 Hero to carry it), Summons (Saga creatures that do something each turn and then leave), Tiered spells (pay more for a bigger effect), Towns, and cards that reward big noncreature spells.
3. **A tight power band with a few memorable bombs.** Mostly cards that won above the format's average, plus a handful of big moments (Summon: Knights of Round, Ardyn, Summon: Bahamut, Ancient Adamantoise) at the top of the curve.
4. **Removal in every colour, and answers to the bombs.** Green fights and eats artifacts; blue bounces and counters.
5. **Two-colour mana.** One tapped Town dual per pair (Balamb Garden is Simic's), plus Capital City, Starting Town, World Map and Blitzball for any colour. Three colours is a stretch on purpose.

## Archetypes

| Pair | Deck | Plan | Signposts |
|---|---|---|---|
| WU | Azorius artifacts and Heroes | Job select Equipment and artifact creatures go wide; Cid pumps them and Tidus grows and taps blockers. | Cid, Timeless Artificer · Tidus, Blitzball Star · Dragoon's Lance · Gaelicat · Delivery Moogle |
| UB | Dimir surveil control | Loot and surveil into the right answers; deathtouch, lifelink and removal hold the board. | Locke Cole · Emet-Selch, Unsundered · Dreams of Laguna · Il Mheg Pixie · Resentful Revelation |
| BR | Rakdos Wizards and spells | Wizard tokens ping on every noncreature spell; Kuja makes one each turn and doubles them at four. | Kuja, Genome Sorcerer · Garland, Knight of Cornelia · Cornered by Black Mages · Circle of Power · Thunder Magic |
| RG | Gruul landfall | Every land drop grows a creature or digs for the next one; Gladiolus fetches a land and pumps the team. | Gladiolus Amicitia · Rydia, Summoner of Mist · Sazh's Chocobo · Ride the Shoopuf |
| WG | Selesnya Summons | Saga creatures do their chapters, Garnet resets them, Yuna gives them trample and lifelink and brings them back. | Yuna, Hope of Spira · Garnet, Princess of Alexandria · Rinoa Heartilly · Summon: Fenrir · Summon: Ixion |
| WB | Orzhov lone attackers | One attacker at a time with double strike; Squall returns cheap permanents from the graveyard when he connects. | Squall, SeeD Mercenary · Rufus Shinra · Zack Fair · Dark Knight's Greatsword |
| BG | Golgari graveyard | Fill the graveyard with permanent cards; Cloud of Darkness and Exdeath get bigger the more there are. | Cloud of Darkness · Jenova, Ancient Calamity · Exdeath, Void Warlock · Town Greeter |
| UG | Simic Towns and ramp | Extra lands and Towns, then Omega stuns their best permanents for as long as you have nonbasic lands. | Ignis Scientia · Omega, Heartless Evolution · Traveling Chocobo · Balamb Garden, SeeD Academy · Prishe's Wanderings |
| UR | Izzet big spells | Noncreature spells with four or more mana spent on them; Tiered spells count when you pay up. | The Emperor of Palamecia · Shantotto, Tactician Magician · Ultros, Obnoxious Octopus · Sahagin · Blazing Bomb |
| WR | Boros Equipment | Job select and Equipment on cheap creatures; Giott loots and Firion copies each new Equipment. | Giott, King of the Dwarves · Zidane, Tantalus Thief · Firion, Wild Rose Warrior · Samurai's Katana · Adelbert Steiner |

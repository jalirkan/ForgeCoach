# The Synergy Cube — version 3 (2026-10-17), 180 cards, two-player

*Designed 2026-10-01; version 3 redesigned 2026-10-16 from the cube lab's evidence, tested in the lab (J137) and shipped on Justin's word on 2026-10-17. All eras, light interaction, no infinite combos, every name verified on Scryfall. Intended as a full-proxy cube, so cost was ignored.*

## Design principles

1. **Nine overlapping themes, not ten separate archetypes.** Nearly every nonland card belongs to two or three themes. A drafted deck is a web, not a pile.
2. **Tight power band.** Every card is solid alone and excellent with friends. No card wins on its own regardless of the board (no Sheoldred, no One Ring, no Ragavan).
3. **Synergy, not combo.** Nothing goes infinite (one three-card exception since v2, under "Cards to watch"). The strongest interactions (Yawgmoth plus fodder, Blood Artist plus a sacrifice outlet, Hardened Scales plus Walking Ballista) win games over a few turns, not on the spot.
4. **Light interaction.** Two soft counters, two cheap hand-attack creatures, plenty of creature removal. Games are decided by what you build.
5. **Mana that supports three colors.** Ten shocklands, ten fetchlands. The fetchlands also feed the landfall and graveyard themes.

## The themes and how they connect

| Code | Theme | Core idea | Connects to |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAC | Sacrifice ("aristocrats") | Fodder plus outlets plus death payoffs | TOK, ART, GY, LIFE, CTR |
| TOK | Tokens / go wide | Makers plus anthems plus things that eat tokens | SAC, ETB, LIFE, SPL |
| ETB | Enter-the-battlefield / blink | Value creatures plus ways to replay them | TOK, GY, LND, ART |
| CTR | +1/+1 counters | Counter makers plus doublers plus proliferate | ART, SAC, LND, LIFE |
| GY | Graveyard | Self-mill, recursion, small reanimation, flashback | SAC, SPL, LND, ETB |
| SPL | Spells matter | Prowess, "whenever you cast", cheap cantrips | TOK, GY, ART |
| ART | Artifacts | Clues, Treasures, Food, cheap artifacts that replace themselves, equipment | SAC, TOK, ETB, CTR |
| LND | Lands / landfall | Fetchlands, land recursion, landfall payoffs | GY, CTR, TOK, ETB |
| LIFE | Lifegain | Incidental gains plus payoffs | SAC, TOK, CTR |

Hub cards that sit in three themes at once: Blood Artist (SAC/TOK/LIFE), Young Pyromancer (SPL/TOK/SAC), Thraben Inspector (ART/ETB/SAC), Tireless Tracker (LND/ART/CTR), Hangarback Walker (CTR/ART/TOK/SAC), Marionette Master (ART/SAC/CTR), Titania (LND/TOK/SAC), Sun Titan (GY/ETB/LND), Grist (TOK/SAC/GY), Third Path Iconoclast (SPL/TOK/ART). When you're drafting, these are the cards that let you change direction without losing what you already picked.

## Decks this cube wants you to find

- **Rakdos or Mardu sacrifice:** Viscera Seer, Goblin Bombardment, Priest of Forgotten Gods, Yawgmoth as outlets; Murderous Redcap as recursive fodder; Young Pyromancer, Ophiomancer, Lingering Souls, Hangarback as fodder; Blood Artist, Zulaport, Mayhem Devil, Midnight Reaper as payoffs.
- **Azorius or Bant blink:** Thraben Inspector, Spirited Companion, Wood Elves, Mulldrifter, Mist Raven, Sower of Temptation, Reflector Mage as targets; Ephemerate, Flickerwisp, Kor Skyfisher, Restoration Angel, Soulherder as the engine; Sun Titan, Serra Paragon and Reveillark on top.
- **Golgari or Abzan counters:** Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Conclave Mentor doubling; Walking Ballista, Hangarback, Pelt Collector, Scavenging Ooze, Evolution Sage with fetchlands.
- **Izzet spells:** cantrips and burn with Swiftspear, Soul-Scar, Young Pyromancer, Third Path Iconoclast, Murmuring Mystic, Talrand; Dreadhorde Arcanist and Snapcaster to replay them; Tolarian Terror and Brazen Borrower as the threats they pay for.
- **Dimir or Sultai graveyard:** Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Thought Scour filling it; Bloodghast, Gravecrawler, Prized Amalgam coming back; Unburial Rites and Persist on Archon of Cruelty or Sun Titan.
- **Artifact aggro (any colors):** cheap artifacts that replace themselves, Cranial Plating, Steel Overseer, Sai, Pia and Kiran, Marionette Master, with Deadly Dispute and Experimental Synthesizer as draw engines.
- **Landfall ramp:** fetchlands with Lotus Cobra, Scute Swarm, Tireless Tracker, Omnath, Avenger, Ramunap Excavator and Titania.
- **Simic lands and graveyard:** Uro, Titan of Nature's Wrath and Risen Reef as the signposts; Satyr Wayfinder, Thought Scour and the fetchlands fill the graveyard Uro escapes from; Ramunap Excavator, Lotus Cobra and Tireless Tracker reward the lands it puts down.
- **Selesnya lifegain tokens:** Soul Warden, Lunarch Veteran, Prosperous Innkeeper, Ajani's Pridemate, Militia Bugler (which finds them), Brimaz, Battle Screech, Conclave Mentor.

## Cards to watch in testing

- **Yawgmoth, Thran Physician** replaced Skullclamp in v2 as the sacrifice engine: it draws a card per creature like Clamp, but costs a life each time and only lives in black. Watch it with persist (Murderous Redcap) and Hangarback Walker.
- **Murderous Redcap + Grumgully, the Generous + any free sacrifice outlet** (Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, Yawgmoth, Goblin Bombardment) loops forever: Grumgully's +1/+1 counter cancels persist's -1/-1 counter, so Redcap comes back every time and deals 2 each. It's the one infinite combo in the cube, three cards across two colours; cut Grumgully if it ever happens.
- **Reanimate** is the cheapest way back for Archon of Cruelty or Sun Titan (turn two after Stitcher's Supplier, Faithless Looting or Cathartic Reunion). Unburial Rites and Persist were the slow versions; watch whether Archon decides games.
- **Hardened Scales / Winding Constrictor** can snowball with Hangarback and Ballista. Intended.
- **Mana Leak and Remand** are the only counterspells. If control decks feel unplayable, add Memory Lapse; if they feel annoying, cut Remand.
- **Chandra** is the closest thing to a standalone bomb now that Elspeth is out (v3); swap it for a hub card if it feels out of place.
- **Sower of Temptation** (v3) is the strongest new card: a Control Magic on a 2/2 flier, and blink makes it steal again. In J137 it was the most-played new card (32% of decks) and the furthest above its colour, +4.8 points over blue's win rate: not a bomb by those numbers, but the one to watch. If it starts deciding games, Mist Raven's bounce is the fallback.
- **Uro, Titan of Nature's Wrath** (v3) needs the AI to escape it; if the lab shows it only ever cast as a three-mana Growth Spiral, it is a weak signpost, not a strong one. In J137 it was played in only 3.6% of decks: so far it is not doing a signpost's job, and a higher-rated Simic card (or the slot back) is the first thing to try in a v4.

## How to draft it

Grid draft (18 grids of 9 from a shuffled 162) is the recommended method: you see the whole pool and can plan a web. Winston works too but hides half the cards. Build 40, with 16–17 lands; the cube's fetchlands and shocks make three colors realistic, so the basic land box should be well stocked.

## Changelog, version 2 → version 3 (shipped 2026-10-17)

Eight swaps; every section keeps its count (White 24, Blue 22, Gold 21) and the mana base is untouched. The reason is the cube lab's J135 run: 1,716 lab drafts of v2 found no single card out of line within its colours, but white decks win 65.4% and blue decks 41.0%, colour by colour, and every pair's rate is what its two colours predict. Two things in the decks account for most of it: each card-flow spell a deck plays (a cantrip, a looter, a cantrip artifact) costs it about 3 points of win rate in the AI's hands, and blue decks carry two more of them than white decks; and white's token payoffs add about 1.5 points per token card. So v3 takes three token payoffs out of white and four card-flow spells out of blue, and puts in board presence for blue and hub cards for white. The numbers in the table are win-rate points: lab decks with the card against decks of the same colour pair without it, over 1,716 drafts. `pc-results/study/synergy-v3-design.md` has the evidence, its intervals and the test.

**The test (J137, 1,500 lab drafts of v2 and of v3 on the same seeds):** pair spread 38.9 → 27.1 points, card flatness 7.95 → 5.89; NOT SHOWN under the strict pre-registered rule (the two spread intervals overlapped by 1.4 points), but every prediction the study made held.
Justin shipped it on 2026-10-17. The full reading is `pc-results/results/J137/summary.md`; the design and its pre-registered test are `pc-results/study/synergy-v3-design.md`.

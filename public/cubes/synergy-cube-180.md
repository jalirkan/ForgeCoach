# The Synergy Cube — version 1 (180 cards, two-player)

*Designed 2026-10-01. All eras, light interaction, no infinite combos, every name verified on Scryfall. Intended as a full-proxy cube, so cost was ignored.*

## Design principles

1. **Nine overlapping themes, not ten separate archetypes.** Nearly every nonland card belongs to two or three themes. A drafted deck is a web, not a pile.
2. **Tight power band.** Every card is solid alone and excellent with friends. No card wins on its own regardless of the board (no Sheoldred, no One Ring, no Ragavan).
3. **Synergy, not combo.** Nothing goes infinite. The strongest interactions (Skullclamp plus tokens, Blood Artist plus a sacrifice outlet, Hardened Scales plus Walking Ballista) win games over a few turns, not on the spot.
4. **Light interaction.** Two soft counters, two cheap hand-attack creatures, plenty of creature removal. Games are decided by what you build.
5. **Mana that supports three colors.** Ten shocklands, ten fetchlands. The fetchlands also feed the landfall and graveyard themes.

## The themes and how they connect

| Code | Theme | Core idea | Connects to |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAC | Sacrifice ("aristocrats") | Fodder plus outlets plus death payoffs | TOK, ART, GY, LIFE, CTR |
| TOK | Tokens / go wide | Makers plus anthems plus things that eat tokens | SAC, ETB, LIFE, SPL |
| ETB | Enter-the-battlefield / blink | Value creatures plus ways to replay them | TOK, GY, LND, ART |
| CTR | +1/+1 counters | Counter makers plus doublers plus proliferate | ART, SAC, LND, LIFE |
| GY | Graveyard | Self-mill, recursion, small reanimation, flashback | SAC, SPL, LND, ETB |
| SPL | Spells matter | Prowess, "whenever you cast", cheap cantrips | TOK, GY, ART |
| ART | Artifacts | Clues, Treasures, Food, cheap artifacts that replace themselves, equipment | SAC, TOK, ETB, CTR |
| LND | Lands / landfall | Fetchlands, land recursion, landfall payoffs | GY, CTR, TOK, ETB |
| LIFE | Lifegain | Incidental gains plus payoffs | SAC, TOK, CTR |

Hub cards that sit in three themes at once: Blood Artist (SAC/TOK/LIFE), Young Pyromancer (SPL/TOK/SAC), Thraben Inspector (ART/ETB/SAC), Tireless Tracker (LND/ART/CTR), Hangarback Walker (CTR/ART/TOK/SAC), Marionette Master (ART/SAC/CTR), Titania (LND/TOK/SAC), Sun Titan (GY/ETB/LND), Grist (TOK/SAC/GY), Third Path Iconoclast (SPL/TOK/ART). When you're drafting, these are the cards that let you change direction without losing what you already picked.

## Decks this cube wants you to find

- **Rakdos or Mardu sacrifice:** Viscera Seer, Goblin Bombardment, Priest of Forgotten Gods as outlets; Young Pyromancer, Ophiomancer, Lingering Souls, Hangarback as fodder; Blood Artist, Zulaport, Mayhem Devil, Midnight Reaper as payoffs.
- **Azorius or Bant blink:** Thraben Inspector, Spirited Companion, Wood Elves, Mulldrifter, Reflector Mage as targets; Ephemerate, Flickerwisp, Restoration Angel, Soulherder as the engine; Sun Titan and Reveillark on top.
- **Golgari or Abzan counters:** Hardened Scales, Winding Constrictor, Conclave Mentor doubling; Walking Ballista, Hangarback, Pelt Collector, Scavenging Ooze, Evolution Sage with fetchlands.
- **Izzet spells:** cantrips and burn with Swiftspear, Soul-Scar, Young Pyromancer, Third Path Iconoclast, Murmuring Mystic, Talrand; Dreadhorde Arcanist and Snapcaster to replay them.
- **Dimir or Sultai graveyard:** Stitcher's Supplier, Satyr Wayfinder, Thought Scour filling it; Bloodghast, Gravecrawler, Prized Amalgam coming back; Unburial Rites and Persist on Archon of Cruelty or Sun Titan.
- **Artifact aggro (any colors):** cheap artifacts that replace themselves, Cranial Plating, Steel Overseer, Sai, Pia and Kiran, Marionette Master, with Deadly Dispute and Skullclamp as draw engines.
- **Landfall ramp:** fetchlands with Lotus Cobra, Scute Swarm, Tireless Tracker, Omnath, Avenger, Ramunap Excavator and Titania.
- **Selesnya lifegain tokens:** Soul Warden, Lunarch Veteran, Prosperous Innkeeper, Ajani's Pridemate, Welcoming Vampire, Adeline, Brimaz, Conclave Mentor.

## Cards to watch in testing

- **Skullclamp** is the one card here that's genuinely too strong in the right deck. It's in because it's the purest expression of the tokens-plus-sacrifice theme; cut it if it dominates.
- **Hardened Scales / Winding Constrictor** can snowball with Hangarback and Ballista. Intended.
- **Mana Leak and Remand** are the only counterspells. If control decks feel unplayable, add Memory Lapse; if they feel annoying, cut Remand.
- **Chandra** and **Elspeth** are the closest things to standalone bombs; swap them for more hub cards if they feel out of place.

## How to draft it

Grid draft (18 grids of 9 from a shuffled 162) is the recommended method: you see the whole pool and can plan a web. Winston works too but hides half the cards. Build 40, with 16–17 lands; the cube's fetchlands and shocks make three colors realistic, so the basic land box should be well stocked.

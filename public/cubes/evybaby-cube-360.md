# Evybaby's New Cube

*Evan's cube: 360 cards, one of each, for Grid drafts with two players (or Winston or booster) and 40-card decks. Added 2026-10-07 so Justin and Evan can grid-draft it together.*

## Source and credit

This is **Evan's cube**, his own list exactly as he gave it: a Cube Cobra-style export named "evybabysNewCube", 360 cards. Nothing was added, cut or swapped. His list has no themes, archetypes or prices, and this document adds none. The headings below are only the colour groups his export already came in, in his order (by colour identity, so Lingering Souls sits in Multicolor and Haywire Mite in Green); the lands are grouped by cycle.

Every name was checked against Scryfall on 2026-10-07. All 360 are exact Scryfall names, so none needed a correction. Double-faced, adventure and prepare cards are listed by their front face, the name Forge uses.

## Not in Forge 2.0.14

Forge 2.0.14 (built 2026-08-08) has no card script yet for these 13 cards, all from Reality Fracture (released 2026-10-02). They draft as normal and a deck exported to another game keeps them. The engine refuses a deck that holds one, so ForgeCoach leaves them out of the AI's deck and off any sideboard it hands the engine, and asks you to take them out of your main deck before a game against Forge. A later Forge release will have them.

## Forge AI flags

The other 347 cards all have a card script in Forge 2.0.14. Forge's AI plays some of them badly, or leaves them out of its decks: their scripts carry `AI:RemoveDeck`. Rated `All`: Prismatic Ending, Divergent Equation, Goblin Welder, Faithless Looting, Goblin Bombardment, Mishra's Bauble. Rated `Random`: Emrakul's Messenger, Micromancer, Nishoba Brawler, Farseek, Witch's Oven, Soulherder, Halo Forager. This only matters when the AI drafts or pilots them.

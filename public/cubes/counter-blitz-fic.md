# Counter Blitz (Final Fantasy X)

*Justin's Final Fantasy X Commander deck (Final Fantasy Commander, FIC, released 2025-06-13), opened 2026-10-10. Not a cube: a 100-card precon he owns, here so the deck assistant can build and score 40-card decks from it, export them, and take them to Play vs Forge. It is not offered for drafting: it is one theme, and a third of it is lands.*

## Source and credit

The decklist is Wizards of the Coast's, as published in the Final Fantasy Commander decklists announcement (https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/announcements/final-fantasy-commander-decklists) and as MTGJSON carries it (`CounterBlitzFinalFantasyX_FIC`). Every name was checked against Scryfall on 2026-10-10. Nothing was added, cut or swapped.

The deck is 100 cards: Tidus, Yuna's Guardian as its commander and 99 others. Commander is singleton, so every card is one of a kind except the basic lands (3 Forest, 3 Island, 3 Plains); those are left out below, because the deck assistant adds basics itself. Three more are left out because they do nothing outside Commander (see *Commander only*, at the end). That leaves the 88 cards listed here, Tidus among them.

It is a green-white-blue +1/+1 counters deck: put counters on creatures, move them (Tidus, Rikku), double or proliferate them (Hardened Scales, Tromell, Lulu, Fathom Mage), and win with a wide board of grown creatures. Twenty-five cards are new to Final Fantasy; the rest are reprints. No themes, archetypes, prices or lab data are written here: the sections are its colour identities, as Evybaby's are.

## Forge

Forge 2.0.14 (built 2026-08-08) is newer than Final Fantasy, so its card scripts should all be there, but nobody has checked this list against the engine yet. Run mtg-table's deck audit on an exported deck before the first game; if it finds cards Forge lacks, list them under a "## Not in Forge 2.0.14" heading here and ForgeCoach will keep them away from the engine.

## Commander only

These three make mana only of a colour in "your commander's color identity". In a 40-card game there is no commander, so they make no mana at all (comprehensive rules 903.4f), and they are not in the list above. Forge of Heroes, whose other ability needs a commander, stays: it still taps for colourless mana.

- Command Tower
- Path of Ancestry

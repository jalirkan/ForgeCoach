# Ordering the test pack: a checklist

For the Dragon Shield custom sleeves (the Sleeve Crafter), standard size.

## Before you upload

- [ ] **Order the smallest pack first.** One colour, or one of each if the
      smallest pack allows it, to see how the print comes out before ordering more.
- [ ] **Pick the standard size** (63 × 88 mm, for Magic cards), not Japanese or
      small size.
- [ ] **Upload the JPEG, one per colour:**
      - green: `two-seats-green-woman.jpg`
      - garnet (red): `two-seats-garnet-man.jpg`
      - blue: `two-seats-blue-man.jpg`

      Each is 1500 × 2100 pixels, 600 dpi, about 1.1 MB. Use the JPEG, not the PNG
      (the green PNG is just over 4 MB, which some uploaders refuse).

## In the Sleeve Crafter's preview

- [ ] **The crop.** The picture should fill the sleeve edge to edge. It is fine if
      the outer plain gold and a little of the gold frame are cut off — they are
      meant to run off the edge. The flowered border should still show all the
      way round.
- [ ] **Any border it adds.** If the crafter puts its own frame or a coloured edge
      round the picture, note how wide it is. If it covers the flowered border,
      try its "no frame" or "full bleed" option if it has one.
- [ ] **The lamp sits on the centre line.** The round glowing lamp should be in the
      exact middle of the sleeve, top to bottom and left to right. If it looks off
      centre, the picture has been shifted or cropped unevenly.
- [ ] **The faces are not cropped.** Both faces — the one right way up and the
      one upside down — should be whole, with the top of each hood clear of the
      edge (there is about 10 mm to spare).
- [ ] **Nothing stretched.** The faces and the round lamp should look the same
      shape as in `CHARACTERS.jpg`; an oval lamp means the picture was stretched.

## When the sleeves arrive

- [ ] **The colour of the gold.** Is it a warm, slightly dull gold like the
      screen, or has it turned brown, green or flat yellow?
- [ ] **The faces at arm's length.** Hold a sleeve at arm's length: can you tell
      the calm woman, the worried man and the guarded man apart? Are the dark
      hoods round the faces still showing detail, or have they gone solid black?
      (The darkest parts of the hair and hood are the one place that might print
      too dark; see `NOTES.md`.)
- [ ] **The hem seam.** Look where the hood's shoulder-cape meets the cloak, just
      below the chin. You should not see a line there.
- [ ] **The props.** Look closely at the table's near edge: the coins and the cup
      (green), the tankard, napkin and one coin (garnet), the knocked-over cup and
      the bitten bun (blue). Can you tell what each one is? They are meant to be
      small and found up close, not seen from across the room.
- [ ] **The cards in their hands** should be plain dark backs with gold edges, no
      marks.
- [ ] **One sleeve with a real card in it.** Does the art show through as
      expected, and does the sleeve shuffle like the others?

Write down anything that looks wrong, with which colour, before ordering more.
